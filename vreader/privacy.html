Privacy Policy

V: ebook reader (Android, package com.devreactor.vreader) · Effective 15 October 2026

V is an ebook reader. It has no servers, no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting services. The developer does not receive any of your data.

Data stored on your device

Your books, reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, notes, collections, reading statistics and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you turn on sync and sign in with your Google account, V uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API to keep your devices in sync. V requests only these permissions:

ScopeWhat V does with it
drive.fileCreates a visible Reader/Books folder in your Drive and stores copies of your book files there; reads and deletes only the files V created or that you open with V. V cannot see your other Drive files.
drive.appdataStores small hidden files in the app's own Drive folder: reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, notes, book statuses, collections, reading sessions, and the name and a random or Android identifier of each of your devices (so another device can offer “Continue from <device>”).
Email, basic profileShows which Google account is connected and keeps sync tied to it.

This data is sent directly from your device to your own Google Drive over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising, profiling, or training models. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy.

V's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: remove a book from the device, or use “Delete everywhere” to remove it from all devices and from Drive; turn off sync in Settings → Sync.
  • In Google Drive: delete the Reader folder; delete V's hidden data in Drive settings → Manage apps → V → Delete hidden app data; revoke V's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other apps

When you tap a link in a book, look up a word, or share text, V hands it to another app you choose (a browser, a dictionary) through Android. That app's own privacy policy applies.

Children

V is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
