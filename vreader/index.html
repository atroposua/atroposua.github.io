V

ebook reader made for foldables

V is an EPUB and FB2 reader for Android, made for foldable phones. It works on regular phones and tablets too.

Foldable phones

Reading is comfortable on both the outer and the inner screen.

Typography

V lays out books itself, so every book looks neat, however the file was made.

Sync

Books, reading progress, bookmarks and notes sync between your devices through your own Google Drive. Sync is optional.

Privacy

No ads, no sign-up, no V servers. Read the privacy policy.
