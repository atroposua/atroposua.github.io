V

ebook reader made for foldables

V reads EPUB and FB2 books. It was made for foldable phones and also works on regular phones and tablets. Reading is comfortable on both the outer and the inner screen.

V lays out every book itself, so books look neat however the file was made.

Books, reading progress, bookmarks and notes can sync between your devices through your own Google Drive. Sync is optional.

V has no ads, no sign-up and no servers of its own. See the privacy policy.
