V

ebook reader made for foldables

V is an ebook reader for Android, designed first for foldable phones and just as comfortable on regular phones and tablets.

Fold and unfold without losing your place

On the cover screen you read one page. Open the phone and that page becomes the left page of a two-page spread. Fold it again and you are exactly where you were.

What it does

  • EPUB and FB2 books laid out by V's own renderer, in one consistent house style
  • Literata, Source Serif and Golos; hyphenation for English, Russian and Ukrainian; Paper, Sepia, Dusk and Night themes
  • Highlights, notes, bookmarks, search in the book and the library
  • Collections, watched folders, “Open with V”, reading statistics
  • Optional sync of books, positions and notes through your own Google Drive

Privacy

No ads, no tracking, no accounts, no V servers. Read the privacy policy.
